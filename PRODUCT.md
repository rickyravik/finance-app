# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User requested Next.js and TypeScript; SQLite chosen for the local MVP.
## Users
One person managing their own UK finances on their own computer.
## Product Purpose
Understand upcoming cash needs and compare housing, travel and investment scenarios with deterministic figures.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Read-only Starling connection, Bank of Scotland manual CSV import, user categorisation rules, recurrence detection, cash-flow planning and local Ollama. No payment initiation or bank login credentials. £0 subscription-first. Sources are read-only.
## Evidence on Hand
The supplied private Bastille brief informs category vocabulary and scenario structure. All seeded amounts and merchants are synthetic. No actual financial data is seeded.
## Open decisions
Confirmed visual preference: light and practical.
